About the game

Hi! I have a daughter named Luna. We love video games and don’t like ads. So we decided to make this game free and ad-free — for everyone, forever. Luna was three and a half when we started making the game, so it’s made for children around that age.

All the places in the game are fictional, but we’ve been to them. If you recognize any of them, it’s purely a coincidence.
